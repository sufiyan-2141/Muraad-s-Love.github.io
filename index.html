A story written for one person.

You.

CHAPTER 01

It started quietly…

Some moments don’t announce themselves as important. They simply happen. And later, you realise you never wanted to forget them.

CHAPTER 02

Then you became familiar.

Not loudly. Not all at once. You just started appearing in the parts of my day that felt better than the rest.

Some people feel like a coincidence. You never did.

CHAPTER 03

The little things stayed.

The way a normal conversation could change my mood. The way a simple photo could make an ordinary day feel less ordinary.

MEMORY JOURNEY

Maybe I was collecting memories
without knowing it.

I kept finding reasons to remember.

A SMALL TEST

How well do you know us?

Before you continue… what do you think I noticed about you first?

WORDS I COULDN’T SAY

Some feelings needed
slower words.

Kuch baatein dil mein reh jaati hain,
kyunki unhe kehne se pehle hi tum samajh jaati ho.

Tumse milkar yeh nahi laga ke zindagi badal gayi,
bas yeh laga ke zindagi mein ek jagah apni si ho gayi.

Main tumhe har roz yaad nahi karta,
tum toh har roz mein hoti ho.

Tumhare saath khamoshi bhi
kabhi khaali nahi lagti.

Tumhara naam aata hai toh
din thoda behtar lagne lagta hai.

ONE MORE THING

Something is hidden here.

Don’t rush. Look carefully.

You found my name.

But you still haven’t found what I’ve been trying to tell you.

There is something I never told you…

I could have written this as a message.

I could have called you.

I could have simply said it.

But somehow…

Three words didn’t feel big enough.

So I built you this.

And then there was this one picture…

The one I kept coming back to.

Because somehow…

this picture feels like you.

ONE LAST QUESTION

You’ve made it this far.

So I think you deserve to know why I made all of this.

Are you ready?

Do you know what my favourite place is?

I have one question left.

Will you be mine? ♥

You just made Muraad
the happiest person alive.

And somehow, every second of making this was worth it.

This isn’t the end of the story.
It’s where our next chapter begins.

I’ll wait.

Some answers are worth waiting for. Take your time.

Just know that somewhere behind this screen, there’s a boy who really, really hopes the answer is yes. ♥

ONE LAST THING…

A note from my heart.

This little world was made only for you. The photos are the memories I keep close, the puzzles are the small ways we find each other, and the shayari is everything that can be hard to say out loud.

And that picture? It is special because it reminds me how lucky I feel to know you.

I could have sent you a message.
I could have called you.
I could have simply said three words.
But you mean more to me than three words.

So I built you a little world instead.
And if you reached this far…
thank you for reading my heart.

— Muraad ♥

THE END?

Maybe not.

Maybe this is where the real story begins.

Made with too many memories, too many feelings, and one question.Muraad ♥
